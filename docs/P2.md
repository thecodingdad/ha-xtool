# xTool P2: cameras, crop presets and overlays

The P2 entities in the [README](../README.md) have these details.

The P2's Overview camera and Close camera are straightened with the P2's own camera calibration (the files Studio reads; the integration only reads them). Positions are in the laser's coordinates, as on Studio's canvas: millimetres from home (0, 0), X to the right and Y toward the front. Home is the top left corner of the Process on slats area, 20.5 mm from the bed's left wall and 40.5 mm from its back wall. "Laser position X/Y" is where the laser fires.

The P2's overview calibration is measured only in the middle of the bed (on the P2 tested: X 60–540, Y 0–280); the rest of its grid is filled in with straight lines, which a wide-angle lens does not follow. The integration finds the measured part, uses the calibration there, and continues the picture outside it with the camera's lens fitted to that part. Checked with a ruled mat, the picture then stays within about 1 mm out to where the camera can see. At slat level the camera does not see the last 15 mm or so at the left wall and 25 mm at the right wall (the side rails hide them).

## Overlays

Each overlay is a switch on the camera's device; any number can be on, and all start off. An area overlay draws its outline with a dot at its origin and one at its center, all in the area's color. The Overview camera has the three Mode areas (Overlay mode process on slats, honeycomb panel, slats removed) and Laser target (where the laser fires; that point is never in the Close camera's picture); the Close camera has Studio and Custom. Both have Slat lines (every slat, odd numbers labeled), Red dot close (a ring as large as the Laser target, the dot visible inside it) and Red dot quick (an outline the dot sits just inside). **Overlay close camera** lays the Close camera's last picture, as that camera shows it (its crop and its own overlays), on the Overview camera where it was taken. Overlay colors are set in the integration's options.

## Overlays select

Each camera has an **Overlays** select (configuration): **Integration** (default) draws the overlays on the picture; **Card** draws none (Overlay close camera is still laid on, as it is a photo), so a dashboard card can draw them in its own style. Either way each overlay switch has two attributes for the picture last taken, left out of the history:

- `position`: where the overlay is, in the same millimetres as that camera's crop numbers (Overview: the laser's, from home; Close: from the center of the close camera's area). Each key holds a list of entries with named values: `areas` (x, y, width, height), `slats` (x0, y0, x1, y1, label), `target` (x, y), `close` (x, y, diameter), `quick` (x, y, length, width, angle in degrees from X toward Y).
- Each camera has a `view` attribute, also left out of the history: the area its last picture shows (x, y, width, height), in the same millimetres, for every Crop.
- `style`: how the integration draws it: `color` [r, g, b], `line` (pixels of the picture), `style` (solid), `icon` (the mark at a point, if any) and `size` (that icon's size in mm).

## Mode and Thickness or distance

Mode (Process on slats, Honeycomb panel, Slats removed) and one Thickness or distance number, read as Studio reads it in that Mode: the material's thickness on the slats, otherwise the distance down from 25 mm above the slats. The overview picture is straightened for that height. **Measure thickness or distance** (with the Measured sensor, on the Overview camera's device, since the result only sets that camera's picture) reads the IR sensor under the close red dot (turning the dot on for the reading if needed) and fills in Thickness or distance; it can be changed after. A reading the selected Mode cannot take (for example with nothing under the red dot) is refused with a message, and Thickness or distance keeps its value.

## Exposure

Each camera's Exposure is 10 to 1000; higher is brighter. The P2 does not report its exposure, so the number shows the value Home Assistant set last. When the Power switch turns the P2 on, Home Assistant sets Studio's values (Overview camera 100, Close camera 150). The number is unknown when the P2 was turned on any other way, until a value is set here; a value Studio sets is not shown. The camera takes a new photo two seconds after a change.

## Moving the laser

Set Laser position X and Y (the numbers show the values set, with the attribute `waiting` until the laser moves), then press **Move laser**. The `xtool.move` action takes the P2 device and moves to X and Y, or by signed steps. The P2 moves the laser while it sleeps too; it moves the laser home a minute before it sleeps and when it wakes.

## Fill lights and settings

Each P2 fill light keeps its own brightness and turns on at it. Studio has one brightness for the right and left lights, so they are kept in step: a change to either writes the brighter of the two to Studio's setting, and a change made in Studio sets both. A settings change made in Studio is not pushed by the P2, so the settings are read on every poll.

## Left out on the P2

Studio does not offer these on the P2, so there is no entity for them: Filter check, Purifier check, Purifier continue (the Exhaust fan setting covers it, as in Studio), Baseplate detection (stored in Studio only), Purifier speed, the cooling fan switch, Time sync and the water line reading. These tell nothing on the P2 and are left out too: Tool type and Working mode (never reported), Gyro X/Y/Z (0 while the laser stands still), Laser power and Laser module (fixed for the model) and Last distance (the Measured sensor has it as its `sensor_distance` attribute). Task ID, CPU temperature, Cooling fan running and Water pump running are disabled by default.
